---
name: Knowing God fidelity
description: Scope and integration decisions for the approved full Knowing God source feature.
---

Knowing God is a faithful integration of the supplied full source feature, not a new interpretation, sample or document-only reconstruction. Preserve the curated Scripture corpus, source identifiers, attributions, browser-local preference/study-list compatibility and complete introduction content.

**Why:** The user approved “Yes—add the full feature” for the uploaded replication source. Its curated corrections and relationships cannot be reproduced by simply extracting the original book again.

**How to apply:** Keep source corpus checksums meaningful when updating the interface. Do not regenerate or replace approved text as an incidental part of a UI change. The NET corpus belongs to the feature itself and must not acquire a runtime dependency on Bible.org or the original site.

The feature may retain its supplied JO EQUIP header and warm reading surfaces, but the rest of Follow Jesus Online must retain its own existing layout and framework.

**Why:** Full-feature parity was requested within the existing app, not as permission for global rebranding or framework replacement.

**How to apply:** Keep feature styles and self-hosted font family names isolated. Keep a route back to Follow Jesus Online, and use explicit external destinations for original hub navigation that is not part of the local app.
